FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about what NexaScan does, and what it doesn't.

Privacy

Does NexaScan upload my documents?

No. Scanning, clean-up, text recognition, conversion and the PDF tools all run on your phone, and the app never uploads your documents or their text. The one thing to know is that Google's text-recognition component may send limited usage and diagnostic information to Google, but never your images or text. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Every feature works without an Internet connection, including text recognition. Its model is built into the app.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Open the app and start scanning.

Where are my files stored?

In NexaScan's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't browse. This storage is left out of Google cloud backups. To keep a copy elsewhere, share the file to Files, Drive or email.

Scanning and text

Does it support OCR (text recognition)?

Yes. NexaScan recognises text in scans, photos and PDFs on your device. You can extract the text, copy or share it, select it straight off the page, and search inside documents. It reads Latin-alphabet languages, such as English, French, German, Spanish and Italian.

Can I create searchable PDFs?

Yes. When you save a scan as a PDF, turn on Make PDF searchable. For an existing scanned PDF, use the Make Searchable PDF tool. The page looks exactly the same, but its words can now be found and copied in any PDF reader.

Can I scan several pages into one document?

Yes. Keep scanning to add pages, up to 200 in one session. Then reorder, rotate, retake or remove pages before you save as one PDF, separate images or one long image.

Files and conversion

What file types are supported?

NexaScan opens PDF, JPG, JPEG and PNG files. It creates PDFs, JPEG images, Word (.docx) and Excel (.xlsx) files. Password-protected PDFs can't be opened.

Can I convert a PDF to Word or Excel?

Yes, from a PDF or a photo. Word conversion keeps paragraphs, headings, tables and pictures where it can. Excel conversion puts each table on its own sheet, with numbers, dates and currency kept as real values. Complex layouts may come out arranged differently, so check important files.

Will a tool change my original file?

No. Merging, compressing, protecting, converting and the other tools always create a new file. Your original is left as it was.

How do I protect a PDF with a password?

Open Password Protect, choose the PDF, and enter a password of at least 8 characters. NexaScan saves an encrypted copy using AES-256. The password isn't stored anywhere, so keep it somewhere safe: it can't be recovered.

Pro and purchases

What is Pro?

NexaScan Pro is an optional monthly or yearly subscription. It adds Word conversion that keeps the exact page layout, Excel conversion for complex tables, and text selection directly on scanned pages. The scanner and PDF toolkit are free. See Pricing.

How do I restore purchases?

Your subscription is tied to your Google Play account. On a new phone, sign in to Google Play with the same account and open NexaScan, and Pro is restored. If it isn't, see Support.

How do I cancel Pro?

In the Google Play app, go to Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, choose NexaScan and cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.
