FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about what NexaScan does, and what it doesn't.

Privacy

Does NexaScan upload my documents?

No. Scanning, clean-up, text recognition, conversion and the PDF tools all run on your phone, and the app never uploads your documents, images or their text. Two small exceptions, neither of which includes your documents: Google's text-recognition component may send limited diagnostic data to Google, and if you buy Pro, the app confirms your subscription with Google Play and our licence service. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Every tool works without an Internet connection, including text recognition, whose model is built into the app. You only need a connection to buy or restore NexaScan Pro.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Open the app and start scanning.

Where are my files stored?

In NexaScan's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't browse. This storage is left out of Google cloud backups. To keep a copy elsewhere, share the file to Files, Drive or email.

Scanning and text

Does NexaScan use AI?

Text recognition uses Google's on-device machine-learning model (ML Kit), which is built into the app and runs on your phone. Page detection and the clean-up looks use image processing that also runs entirely on your phone.

Does it support OCR (text recognition)?

Yes. NexaScan recognises text in scans, photos and PDFs on your device. On Free you can extract the text or make a searchable PDF, up to 10 pages at a time. Pro lifts that limit and adds the OCR Viewer, where you select and copy text straight off scanned pages. Recognition reads Latin-alphabet languages, such as English, French, German, Spanish and Italian.

Can I create searchable PDFs?

Yes, on Free and Pro. When you save a scan as a PDF, turn on Make PDF searchable. For an existing scanned PDF, use the Searchable PDF tool. The page looks the same, but its words can now be found and copied in any PDF reader.

Can I scan several pages into one document?

Yes. Keep scanning to add pages, then reorder, rotate, retake or remove them before you save. Free saves up to 10 pages per document; Pro allows up to 200 pages in one scan.

Does NexaScan add a watermark to my files?

No. Your files never carry a NexaScan watermark or logo, on Free or Pro.

Files and conversion

What file types are supported?

NexaScan opens PDF, JPG, JPEG and PNG files. It creates PDFs, JPEG images, Word (.docx) and Excel (.xlsx) files, and with Pro, one long image of every page. Password-protected PDFs can't be opened.

Can I convert a PDF to Word or Excel?

Yes, from a PDF or a photo, up to 10 pages at a time on Free. Word conversion rebuilds paragraphs, headings and tables; Excel puts tables on sheets with numbers and dates kept as values. Pro adds Word Pro and Excel Pro, which follow the original layout and complex tables more closely. Complex pages may still come out arranged differently, so check important files.

Will a tool change my original file?

No. Merging, compressing, protecting, converting and the other tools always create a new file. Your original is left as it was.

How do I protect a PDF with a password?

Password protection is part of NexaScan Pro. Open Protect, choose the PDF and enter a password of at least 8 characters. NexaScan saves an encrypted copy using AES-256. The password isn't stored anywhere, so keep it somewhere safe: it can't be recovered.

Free, Pro and purchases

What can I do for free?

Scan documents, clean them up with five looks, recognise text, make searchable PDFs, sign, merge, split, compress, organise pages, and convert to Word, Excel and images. Free jobs have limits: 10 pages per document or job, 3 files per merge and 50 MB for compression.

What does Pro add?

Pro lifts the Free limits and adds six more clean-up looks, the OCR Viewer, Word Pro and Excel Pro, Edit PDF, watermarks, page numbers, multi-page sheets, one long image, and password protection. It's a monthly or yearly subscription through Google Play. See Pricing.

How do I restore purchases?

Your subscription is tied to your Google Play account. On a new phone, sign in to Google Play with the same account, open NexaScan, go to Settings › NexaScan Pro and tap Restore purchases. If Pro still doesn't appear, see Support.

How do I cancel Pro?

In the Google Play app, go to Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, choose NexaScan and cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.
