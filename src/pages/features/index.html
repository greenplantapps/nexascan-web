Features

Everything your documents need, in one app

NexaScan brings scanning, clean-up, text recognition, conversion and PDF tools together. You don't need a different app for each step.

Capture

A camera scanner that finds the page, straightens it and builds multi-page documents.

  • Live page detection
  • Auto Capture and torch
  • Crop, rotate and reorder pages
  • Import photos and PDFs
  • Save as PDF, images or one long image

Clean up

One-tap looks that remove shadows, whiten paper and sharpen faint print.

  • Enhance and Light
  • No Shadow
  • B&W, Grayscale and Eco
  • Apply to every page at once
  • Compare with the original

Read On-device AI

Built-in text recognition turns pictures of words into words you can search, copy and share.

  • Make scanned PDFs searchable
  • Extract text from photos and PDFs
  • Select text across pages
  • Search inside documents
  • Works offline

Convert

Get your content out of a PDF and into the format the next person needs.

  • PDF or photo to Word (.docx)
  • PDF or photo to Excel (.xlsx)
  • PDF to images
  • Photos to PDF

Organise PDFs

Reshape documents page by page, without a computer.

  • Merge PDFs
  • Split and extract pages
  • Rotate, reorder and delete pages
  • Compress PDF
  • Several pages on one sheet

Sign & protect

Finish documents and control who can open them.

  • Draw a signature, add text
  • Highlight and pixelate
  • Text or logo watermarks
  • Page numbers
  • AES-256 password protection

In the app

Designed to be quick to use

Big, clear controls, the tools you use most on the home screen, and a search that finds any tool by what you want to do.

The details

Supported files and limits
OpensPDF, JPG, JPEG and PNG. Files can also arrive from other apps through Open with and Share.
CreatesPDF, JPEG images, one long image, Word (.docx) and Excel (.xlsx).
Text recognitionRuns on your phone and works offline. It reads Latin-alphabet languages, such as English, French, German and Spanish.
Password protectionAES-256 encryption on a new copy of the PDF. Your password is not stored.
Your originalsTools create a new file. The file you started from is left unchanged.
LimitsUp to 200 pages in a scanning session. PDFs up to 500 MB. Password-protected PDFs can't be opened.
PlatformAndroid phones.
