How it works

From a sheet of paper to a finished file

Four steps, all on your phone.

  1. Scan

    Point your camera at a page. NexaScan finds the edges, captures when the page is steady, and straightens the shot.

  2. Improve

    Enhance whitens the paper and sharpens the text for you. Try another look, or compare with the original.

  3. Read

    Save it as a searchable PDF, and the words in your scan can be found, copied and shared.

  4. Share

    Keep it as a PDF or images, convert it to Word or Excel, and send it to any app.

Questions

Good to know

All questions and answers

Does NexaScan upload my documents?

No. Everything you do to a document happens on your phone. If you buy Pro, the app confirms your subscription with Google Play and our licence service, which never receive your documents. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Every tool works without a connection, including text recognition. You only need one to buy or restore Pro.

Which phones does it run on?

Android phones running Android 8.0 or later.
