How billing works

  • Subscribe in the app, monthly or yearly. Google Play handles payment, renewal and refunds, and we never see your card details.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically. Cancel at any time in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions, and keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • Your subscription belongs to your Google account, so you can restore it on a new phone with Restore purchases.
  • Any introductory offer is shown in Google Play before you confirm.
  • Upgrading doesn't change how your documents are handled: they are still processed on your phone.
