Why upgrade?

Free already converts documents to Word and Excel. It rebuilds the content as clean paragraphs and simple tables, which is ideal when you mainly need the words. Pro goes further for the documents that matter most:

  • Contracts, forms and reports come out looking like the original, with the layout, columns, headers and pictures where you expect them, and still fully editable.
  • Invoices, statements and spreadsheets keep complex tables intact, so you can edit the figures instead of retyping them.
  • Scanned documents become text you can select, copy and share directly from the page.

Pro, like everything else in NexaScan, runs on your phone. Upgrading doesn't send your documents anywhere.

How billing works

  • Subscribe in the app. Google Play handles payment, renewal and refunds, and we never see your card details.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically. Cancel at any time in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions, and keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • Your subscription is linked to your Google account, so you can restore it on a new phone.
  • Any free trial or introductory offer is shown in Google Play before you confirm.
