Privacy Policy

Your documents are yours. This page explains, in plain language, what NexaScan does with them and what it doesn't.

Effective {{config:policyDate}}. Applies to the NexaScan app for Android.

The short version

  • Scanning, clean-up, text recognition, conversion and every PDF tool run on your phone.
  • NexaScan does not upload your documents, images or their text to us or to anyone else. We don't run servers that receive them.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.
  • Your documents are kept in the app's private storage and are left out of Google cloud backups.
  • A document leaves the app only when you share it or open it in another app.
  • One exception to know about: the text-recognition component from Google (ML Kit) may send limited usage and diagnostic data to Google. This never includes your images or document text. Details are below.

2. What the app works with

To do its job, NexaScan works with the following on your device:

  • Camera images, when you scan a document.
  • Files you choose: PDFs and images (JPG, JPEG, PNG) you pick with Android's file picker, or send to NexaScan from another app with Open with or Share.
  • The documents it creates: PDFs, images, Word and Excel files, and the text it recognises in them.
  • Passwords you type in Password Protect. They are used once to encrypt the new copy, and are never saved or logged.
  • A few app settings, such as whether you've seen the introduction screens.

All of this stays on your device. We don't receive it.

3. Data we collect

We do not collect personal data. NexaScan has no account, and it doesn't send your documents, images, recognised text, file names or contacts to us. It also has no advertising or analytics SDK of our own. The app itself makes no network requests.

5. Data sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your data, because we don't have it. When you tap Share or Open, Android passes the chosen file to the app you pick. What happens next is governed by that app's privacy policy.

7. Where your documents are stored

  • Documents you save are kept in NexaScan's private app storage. Other apps can't read this area, and it is protected by your phone's own security and encryption.
  • This document storage is excluded from Google cloud backup. On Android 12 and later, a direct device-to-device transfer to a new phone may copy it.
  • Working files are kept in the app's private cache while a task runs, and are deleted automatically afterwards. Copies made for sharing are removed within about a day.
  • NexaScan doesn't save documents to your gallery or shared folders. They leave the app only when you share or open them.

8. Retention and deletion

Your documents stay on your device until you delete them. You can delete any document in the app. Uninstalling NexaScan, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, removes all its documents and settings from the device. We hold no copy, so there's nothing for us to delete on a server.

9. Security

  • Processing happens on the device, so your documents don't travel over the Internet when you use NexaScan.
  • Password Protect encrypts a new copy of the PDF with AES-256. Your original file is unchanged, and your password isn't stored.
  • The Password Protect screen blocks screenshots while you type.
  • NexaScan doesn't add its own encryption to documents in its storage. They rely on Android's app sandbox and device encryption. For sensitive files, use Password Protect.

10. Children

NexaScan is a general-purpose document tool. It isn't directed at children, and it doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

11. Purchases

NexaScan Pro is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy, and we never receive your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play whether your account has an active subscription, so that it can unlock Pro features. Buying Pro doesn't change how your documents are handled: they stay on your device.
