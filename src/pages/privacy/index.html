Privacy Policy

Your documents are yours. This page explains, in plain language, what NexaScan does with your data and what it doesn't.

Effective {{config:policyDate}}. Applies to the NexaScan app for Android.

The short version

  • Scanning, clean-up, text recognition, conversion and every PDF tool run on your phone.
  • NexaScan never uploads your documents, images or the text in them, to us or to anyone else.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • Your documents are kept in the app's private storage and are left out of Google cloud backups. They leave the app only when you share them.
  • If you buy NexaScan Pro, the app checks your subscription with Google Play and with our licence service. This uses a purchase token and an identifier for your installation, never your documents or your name (section 5).
  • Google's text-recognition component (ML Kit) may send limited diagnostic data to Google. It never includes your images or text (section 4).

2. What the app works with on your phone

  • Camera images, when you scan a document.
  • Files you choose: PDFs and images (JPG, JPEG, PNG) you pick with Android's file picker, or send to NexaScan from another app with Open with or Share.
  • The documents it creates: PDFs, images, Word and Excel files, and the text it recognises in them.
  • Passwords you type in Protect. They are used once to encrypt the new copy, and are never saved or logged.
  • A few app settings, such as whether you've seen the introduction screens.

All of this stays on your phone. We never receive it.

3. What we collect

We don't collect your documents, images, recognised text, file names or contacts, and NexaScan has no advertising or analytics SDK. The only data that reaches us is the licence data described in section 5, and only if you buy NexaScan Pro.

5. Purchases and the licence check

NexaScan Pro is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment, and we never receive your card or payment details.

After a purchase, the app confirms the subscription with our licence service so that Pro can be unlocked, kept active and restored. For this, the app sends:

  • the purchase token that Google Play issues for your subscription;
  • an installation identifier, derived from a key that the app creates on your phone, together with a signature proving the request comes from this installation.

The licence service doesn't receive your name, email address, documents or their text, and you don't need an account. It runs on Amazon Web Services in the United Kingdom (London region). [LICENCE DATA RETENTION REQUIRED]

If you never buy Pro, the app doesn't contact the licence service at all.

6. Data sharing

We don't sell or rent your data. When you tap Share or Open, Android passes the chosen file to the app you pick, and that app's privacy policy applies. The licence data in section 5 is processed by Amazon Web Services on our behalf, and Google Play handles purchases under its own terms.

8. Where your documents are stored

  • Documents you save are kept in NexaScan's private app storage. Other apps can't read this area, and it is protected by your phone's own security and encryption.
  • This storage is excluded from Google cloud backup. On Android 12 and later, a direct device-to-device transfer to a new phone may copy it. The installation identifier and licence information are excluded from both.
  • Working files are kept in the app's private cache while a task runs and are deleted afterwards. Leftover temporary files are removed after about a day.
  • NexaScan doesn't save documents to your gallery or shared folders. They leave the app only when you share or open them.

9. Retention and deletion

Your documents stay on your phone until you delete them. You can delete any document in the app, and uninstalling NexaScan or clearing its storage in Android Settings removes all its documents and settings. We hold no copy of your documents. To ask about or delete licence data linked to your installation, email {{email:privacyEmail}}.

10. Security

  • Processing happens on the phone, so your documents don't travel over the Internet when you use NexaScan.
  • Protect (Pro) encrypts a new copy of the PDF with AES-256. Your original file is unchanged, and your password isn't stored. The Protect screen blocks screenshots while you type.
  • NexaScan doesn't add its own encryption to documents in its storage. They rely on Android's app sandbox and device encryption. For sensitive files, use Protect.

11. Children

NexaScan is a general-purpose document tool. It isn't directed at children, and it doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
