Troubleshooting

The scanner doesn't find the page edges

  • Put the page on a surface that contrasts with it, such as white paper on a dark table.
  • Make sure all four corners are in view, with a little space around them.
  • Turn on the torch in dim light, and avoid strong glare.
  • You can always adjust the crop yourself by dragging the frame.

The camera doesn't open

NexaScan needs camera permission to scan. If you declined it, go to Android Settings › Apps › NexaScan › Permissions and allow Camera. You can still import photos and PDFs without it.

A PDF won't open

Password-protected or restricted PDFs can't be opened, even when you know the password. A damaged file may also fail to open. Very large PDFs, over 500 MB, aren't supported.

"Limit reached" or "Pro feature"

Free jobs have limits: 10 pages per document or job, 3 files per merge, and 50 MB for compression. Split a bigger job into smaller ones, or upgrade to NexaScan Pro, which lifts these limits. Tools with a Pro badge need NexaScan Pro. See Pricing for the full list.

Recognised text has mistakes

Text recognition works best on sharp, well-lit, straight pages. Try rescanning with the Enhance or B&W look. Recognition reads Latin-alphabet languages, so other scripts, such as Chinese, Arabic or Hindi, aren't recognised.

A converted Word or Excel file looks different from the original

Conversion rebuilds the content as editable paragraphs and tables. The text and tables come across, but complex layouts may be arranged differently. Check important documents after converting.

Where are my files?

Everything you save is in the app, on the Documents tab. To put a file somewhere else, open it and tap Share, then pick a destination such as Files, Drive or email.

Purchases and restoring them

NexaScan Pro is managed by Google Play and linked to your Google account.

  • Restore on a new phone: sign in to Google Play with the account you subscribed with, open NexaScan, then go to Settings › NexaScan Pro and tap Restore purchases.
  • Pro not showing? Check that Google Play is signed in to the right account and that the phone is online, then tap Restore purchases again.
  • Manage or cancel: go to Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. Uninstalling doesn't cancel a subscription.
  • Refunds: Google Play handles refunds under its own refund policy.

Moving to a new phone

For your privacy, documents aren't included in Google cloud backups. Before switching phones, share the files you want to keep to a location you control.
